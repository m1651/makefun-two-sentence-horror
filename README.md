# Two-Sentence Horror

A make.fun bounty entry. `main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes
an original two-sentence horror story — a normal scene, then a twist — to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> My daughter drew our family on the fridge again: four smiling stick figures, the dog, the sun in the corner. I counted the figures twice before I realised there were five, and the fifth one was holding my hand.

The story is 210 characters, deterministic, and validated before writing
(non-empty, single line, exactly two sentences, within the 400-character limit).
